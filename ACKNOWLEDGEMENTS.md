# Acknowledgements

Thank you to the players, testers and contributors who tried Litch on real boards,
reported problems and helped document how to play and build it.

Litch uses Flutter, CocoaPods, PlatformIO and Arduino, along with libraries
including mcu-max, ArduinoJson, NeoPixelBus, NimBLE-Arduino and flutter_reactive_ble.
Thank you to their maintainers and to SparkFun for the VEML6030 ambient-light library.

The [dependency inventory](docs/release/generated/dependency_inventory.md) lists
the components and versions. The [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
credit their authors and provide the license terms.
