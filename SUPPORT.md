# Support

In the Litch app, open **Contact Support** to prepare an email to [support@foxieproducts.com](mailto:support@foxieproducts.com). Review or edit the message and attachments in your mail app before sending. Include your app version, board revision and the steps that led to the problem.

For a suspected security issue, follow the [private reporting instructions](SECURITY.md). The [privacy policy](https://foxieproducts.com/policies/privacy-policy) explains how Foxie handles support messages.
