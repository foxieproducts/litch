# Foxie Litch App Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy covers the Foxie Litch app for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Foxie Products, LLC. You can use the app without an account. You can review saved games and create Litchcraft artwork offline. The app has no ads or third-party usage analytics.

## Connecting to your board

The app uses Bluetooth to find nearby Litch boards. It reads advertised names and device identifiers, and remembers a board so you can reconnect. Pairing lets a Litch board recognize an authorized device. Your device and board exchange settings, game positions and moves, artwork, and board health over Bluetooth. Your device’s Bluetooth system also manages pairing records.

On Android, the app requests location access while in use as part of Bluetooth discovery. It does not read or record your geographic coordinates. On iPhone and iPad, the app requests Bluetooth access to find and connect to the board. You can change these permissions in your device settings.

## What your device keeps

The app uses its private storage on your device to keep preferences, board identifiers, local player profiles and names you enter, saved games and moves, and Litchcraft artwork and drafts. This lets it remember your board, show your game library, and reopen your artwork. Post-game analysis runs on your device. When you use shake-to-erase, the app processes motion readings there too.

For troubleshooting, the app keeps a rolling local record of recent app activity. A separate, limited Console history can contain board identifiers and the text displayed in the Console. The app does not automatically upload these records. Your device’s backup or transfer settings can affect where copies of app data are kept.

## Firmware updates

The app can check for board firmware automatically, even when you have not chosen to install an update. It requests release information and notes from updates.foxieproducts.com over HTTPS. If you choose to install an update, the app uses a verified copy on your device or downloads the firmware, then transfers it to your board over Bluetooth.

Amazon Web Services delivers these files for Foxie. It receives the network information needed to serve a request, including your IP address, the time and the file requested. The request can show whether you selected the standard or beta release channel. It contains no board identifier, player name, game history or artwork.

We do not log individual firmware requests or IP addresses during routine operation. Any routine monitoring is limited to aggregate download and error counts. If we need detailed request logs to investigate a specific incident, we turn logging on only for that investigation and delete each entry within seven days of collection.

## Contacting support

Contact Support prepares an editable report on your device. It can include app and operating system versions, Bluetooth permission status, board firmware, recent actions and errors. When your board provides diagnostic details, the report can also show the game phase and which squares have pieces on them. The app leaves board identifiers, Console history and artwork out of the report. You can read, change or remove any line before sharing it.

If you choose Open email draft, the app passes the report to your mail app. You decide whether to send it. You can also copy the report yourself. If the app cannot open an email draft, it copies the report to your clipboard. If you send an email, Foxie and its email provider receive your email address, the report you choose to send and anything else you include. We use your message to respond and troubleshoot the issue.

We keep your support email and any attachments while your case is open. We delete them within 12 months after we close your case.

## Importing, sharing and opening links

When you import artwork, the app reads the file you choose in your device’s file picker. When you share artwork, the app passes the selected artwork or image to your device’s share sheet. The app or person you choose can keep a copy. Opening a website from Foxie Litch takes you to that site in your browser, where that site’s privacy policy applies.

## Removing data and contacting us

You can delete individual games and saved artwork from their libraries. For a game copied from a board, the app keeps a small record of the deletion so it can skip that game on the next sync. Deleting a saved artwork can leave the open canvas as a draft. To remove the app’s local data, use Clear storage on Android or Delete App on iPhone or iPad. Manage board copies, Bluetooth pairings, device backups, exported files and sent email separately.

For a privacy question or to ask Foxie to delete a support message, email support@foxieproducts.com. If you are asking about a past message, its date or subject helps us find it. You do not need to attach a diagnostic report.
