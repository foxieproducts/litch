## 1.0.5

- With Stored artwork selected, an empty board now plays your saved animation once at startup, then returns to Play or Litchcraft automatically.
- Light-square brightness now ranges from a faint glow at 1 to a brighter maximum at 8. Level 4 matches the previous default. The steps are easier to see in both White and Color, even with automatic brightness on.

## 1.0.4

- Pair another phone even when old Bluetooth addresses have filled the board’s memory. Clear Pairings and Factory Reset remove saved phone keys and addresses.
- Identify and Identify All return to the game display when the board numbers expire.
- Undo dims the board and highlights the piece to move back.
- Capture hints keep pulsing on the piece to remove when you request the same hint again.

## 1.0.3

- Improved movement animation cues.

## 1.0.2

Initial release

Play Classic or Duck Chess with a friend or against 11 computer levels. Lift a piece to see its legal moves light up. Ask for a hint, take back a move, or play with the built-in chess clock.

Use the Foxie Litch app to review saved games, adjust the board lights, and display your own Litchcraft artwork and animations.
